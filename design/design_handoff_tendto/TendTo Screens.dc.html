Why local-first feels different
There is a particular quiet to software that doesn't wait for anything. You press a key and the letter is simply there — no spinner, no optimistic ghost, no debounced save. The document was always yours; the server just gets a copy eventually.
Most sync engines invert this. They treat the cloud as the source of truth and your machine as a cache. TendTo does the opposite.
The network is an optimization, not a requirement.
